# Pilot proposal

Apply the methodology to one real and bounded User Story.

## Pilot deliverables

1. Output-nature classification.
2. Requirement and risk traceability.
3. DoD, DoT and UAT scenarios as applicable.
4. A small reviewed evaluation dataset.
5. At least one deterministic evaluator.
6. At least one business-quality evaluator where needed.
7. Approved thresholds, slices and vetoes.
8. A versioned execution manifest.
9. One baseline run and one candidate comparison.
10. A documented Pass, Fail or Not Ready report.
11. A first version of the design or execution skill.

## Pilot success criteria

The pilot is successful if the team can:

- reproduce the same evaluation protocol;
- explain every gate and failure;
- trace every scenario to a requirement or risk;
- identify regressions by case and critical slice;
- prevent the agent from guessing missing decisions;
- preserve a human-owned acceptance decision.

> **Takeaway:** Start with one bounded use case and prove the complete evidence chain before scaling the framework.
