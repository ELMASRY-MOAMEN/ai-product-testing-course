## Mastery criterion

Given the results of a campaign, you compute its figures, write a status that **starts with what is not done**, and tell the decision owner which exit criterion is not met, with its evidence, without lowering the criterion or taking the decision yourself.

If you cannot, reread [note 8](where-do-we-stand.md), the section *What each figure hides*.
