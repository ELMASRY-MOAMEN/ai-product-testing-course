**"What closes it"** is a specific, obtainable thing: *a written confirmation of the minimum from the product owner*, not *agreement on the approach*.

## Ownership, checked

Common misattributions — confirm none of these is in your table:

| Decision | Belongs to | Not to |
|---|---|---|
| Threshold, slice minimum, acceptance policy | Product owner | QA, engineering, you |
| What "correct" means for a case | Business expert / annotator | QA, you |
| Whether a condition is unacceptable | Product owner, on Compliance's advice | Engineering |
| Regulatory reading of a requirement | Compliance / Legal | Product owner alone |
| Whether production data may be used, and how | Data protection, with Legal | The team, informally |
| Accepting residual risk and releasing | Authorized decision owner | The runner, the report, you |
